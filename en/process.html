How It Works

A simple process, even if you've never done this before

Most EU SMBs I work with have never worked with a CRO consultant. Here's exactly what happens, step by step, so there are no surprises.

1

Audit

I review your site against conversion, UX, and messaging best practices — analytics, user behavior, competitor positioning, and copy. Typical length: 3–5 business days.

2

Findings

You get a written report ranked by impact and effort, plus a 30-minute walkthrough call in your preferred language. No jargon — just what to fix and why.

3

Fix & test

I implement changes directly or hand off clear specs to your developer, then validate results with A/B testing or before/after analysis. Typical length: 2–4 weeks.

4

Results

A plain-language results report: what changed, what it moved, and what to do next — whether that's another sprint or handing things back to your team.
